# BudgetSight message experiment

## Hypothesis
A credibility-led message that emphasizes a clear, dependable budget view will earn more qualified demo interest from cautious budgeters. A speed-led message that promises a quick check-in will earn more initial engagement from people who value low effort. Compare both against the same product preview and audience source.

## Audience
People actively trying to manage a personal or household monthly budget, split across the two variants at random. Keep acquisition source, device mix, and campaign context balanced; exclude internal traffic.

## Variants
- **Credibility:** “Clarity you can plan around.” Leads with confidence and a transparent sample dashboard.
- **Speed:** “Your budget. At a glance.” Leads with a fast check-in and a direct look at the sample dashboard.

## Success metric
Primary: demo CTA click-through rate per unique landing-page visitor. Track completed demo starts as a guardrail so a higher click rate does not mask lower follow-through. Run until each arm has adequate traffic for a preselected minimum detectable lift; review by device as a secondary cut.

## Practical notes
Use identical traffic sources and measurement windows. The sample dashboard labels its values illustrative; do not present them as customer results. Record the CTA event consistently across both pages before launch.
